# Dictator game

The Ultimatum game with the rejection removed. Whatever the allocator keeps, it
keeps, so nothing strategic is left and what remains is disposition alone. That
is why it is the cleanest of the three for Horton's method, and why it appears
in the defence slides through his replications.

Run it beside `ultimatum.md` with the same endowment and the same wording. The
difference between what a model offers here and what it offers as a proposer
there separates what it gives from what it gives in order not to be refused.
Reporting either number on its own says very little.

---

You have been given 100 points to divide between yourself and one other player.

You decide how to split them. Whatever you allocate to each of you is what each
of you receives. The other player makes no decision and cannot refuse.

You decide once.

Answer in exactly this format, and nothing else:

```
OFFER: <a whole number from 0 to 100, the points the other player receives>
REASON: <one or two sentences>
```
